### Google Search ranking hub
The Google Search ranking hub is your central place to learn how Google ranks search results and to keep up with changes to our ranking systems.
## Our principles for ranking results
We rank all types of results based on their usefulness to the user's query. We don't design our ranking systems or AI models to favor any specific site's interests, and we never accept payment to rank web pages higher in organic search results. Every part of our system is guided by three principles.
