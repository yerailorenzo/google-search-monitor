# Submit feedback about ranking changes in Google Search
Website owners, publishers, and creators can submit feedback about ranking updates in Google Search or request dispute resolution through the channels on this page.
## Submit feedback on a ranking update
When Google rolls out a significant ranking change or actionable policy update on the Search Status Dashboard, verified site owners can submit feedback directly in Google Search Console by clicking the Submit Feedback button. In the feedback box, include the specific actionable policy change or significant ranking change, in hashtag format (for example, if it was a November core update 2026, include #NovemberCoreUpdate2026 in the submission).
It's helpful to be as detailed as possible in your feedback. We recommend including the following details:
- Performance report snapshot: A snapshot of the performance report
- Search queries and locale: Exact query terms, country, and language where the issue appears
- Example URLs: Your affected URLs alongside competing URLs that appear to rank inappropriately (for example, scraped content outranking an original source)
- Impact: Information about when and how your URLs were impacted (for example, a screenshot of traffic change if relevant)
- Context: Why the current results fail to serve searchers, along with more detailed before-and-after Search Console impressions or position data (if relevant)
- UK market impact: How the change impacted the functioning of a UK market in which you are active
Important: Engineering teams review submissions in aggregate to identify systemic issues and make Search better for everyone. Submitting feedback doesn't result in manual ranking adjustments for individual URLs.
## Request dispute resolution
If you've received an action on your site and meet the conditions for the appropriate category (described below), you can appeal and, in some cases, request dispute resolution:
- Manual action and spam reconsideration requests: If you've received a manual action for violating Google's spam policies, you can see the details in the Manual actions report in Search Console. After you fix the issues on all affected pages, you can request a review (also known as a reconsideration request) directly in the report. We'll send you a confirmation message when the review is complete.
- Counterfeit goods reconsideration requests: If you're a website owner and you believe that your web page has been incorrectly removed for violation of our Google Search counterfeit goods policy, you can submit an appeal.
- SafeSearch reconsideration requests: If you believe your site is incorrectly flagged as explicit in Google Search results, follow our troubleshooting guide. If you applied fixes, wait at least 2-3 months before requesting a review, as it can take up to 2-3 months for our classifiers to re-process your content. If your site has always followed the guidance for optimizing your site, you may request a review immediately.
- DMCA / copyright counter notice: If your content has been removed from a Google product due to a copyright removal request, and you believe there has been an error or you have the right to use the claimed material, you may file an appeal, also known as a counter notice.
- UK CMA indexing appeal: If your site provides web content, goods, or services to users in the United Kingdom and your site as a whole has been manually excluded from Google's search index, you will be able to submit an appeal using this link starting on December 17, 2026. This link won't become active until December 17, 2026.
- EU DMA appeals: If your site is in the European Economic Area (EEA) and you believe you've met the Google Search DMA access conditions but your site is not appearing in Search, we recommend you first verify your website in Search Console and look for errors in indexing or other problems. We also offer an appeals process under Article 6(12) of the Digital Markets Act (DMA).
## Other reporting and support channels
- Report spam: Report sites that are violating Google Search spam policies.
- Google Search Central Community: Ask questions and troubleshoot with Product Experts and the Search team.
